Quick Support FAQ

Click a question to see the answer.

Do you support Power Apps? +
Yes. Support includes Canvas Apps, Model-driven Apps and related Power Platform customization and troubleshooting.
Can you help with Power Automate failures? +
Yes. Complex flows, failed runs, expressions, connectors and integration-related issues can be reviewed.
Do you handle Dynamics 365 client-side customization? +
Yes. JavaScript, form events, client-side logic and related Dynamics 365 customization issues are included.
Is monthly support available? +
Yes. Monthly technical support is available for teams that need ongoing Dynamics 365 and Power Platform troubleshooting, maintenance, enhancements and development support. Contact with your expected support requirements.
Can I contact you for a single requirement? +
Yes. Requirement-based support is available for individual bugs, enhancements, JavaScript, plugins, Power Automate, Power Apps, PCF, SSRS, Azure Functions, integrations and other Dynamics 365 technical requirements.
Can I contact you for a complex or urgent issue? +
Yes. Send the issue details by email and include the technology, error/problem description and urgency.
